# Houshyar brand assets

- `mark.svg`: primary standalone brand symbol; transparent canvas.
- `wordmark-dark.svg`: wordmark for dark surfaces.
- `wordmark-light.svg`: wordmark for light surfaces.
- `/favicon.svg`: browser favicon and compact app mark.

## Identity

The original Houshyar symbol combines an abstract continuous loop (ongoing intelligence and assistance), a central axis (the H), and four neural connection nodes. The geometry and artwork are original to Houshyar and do not incorporate third-party AI company logos.

Palette: midnight navy `#0C1729`, aqua `#75F0D1`, blue `#48BFEA`, violet `#9A83FF`.
