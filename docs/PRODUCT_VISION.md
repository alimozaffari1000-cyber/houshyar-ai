# Houshyar — Human-Centered Product Vision

## Mission

Houshyar exists to help people meet everyday and long-term needs, reduce avoidable effort, and live safer, healthier, more informed, and more comfortable lives.

It is not merely a chatbot or a collection of AI features. It is a human-centered service platform that helps people understand a need, compare trustworthy options, and complete useful tasks with their informed control.

## Product principles

1. **Real-world usefulness:** Prioritize measurable improvements in time, cost, access, effort, and quality of life over engagement metrics.
2. **Human agency:** Explain recommendations, offer meaningful choices, and require explicit confirmation for consequential actions such as purchases, payments, publishing, deletion, or deployment.
3. **Safety and trust:** Protect personal data, minimize collection, disclose uncertainty and limitations, and do not present AI output as a substitute for qualified professionals in high-stakes domains.
4. **Accessibility and inclusion:** Design for different languages, abilities, literacy levels, devices, and levels of connectivity.
5. **Interoperability:** Integrate useful services and AI providers through replaceable adapters; avoid unnecessary vendor lock-in.
6. **Reliability:** Test critical user journeys, monitor service health without exposing secrets or personal content, and make failures understandable and recoverable.
7. **Fair value:** Make pricing and commercial relationships transparent. Sponsored or paid placement must not masquerade as neutral recommendations.
8. **Privacy by design:** Collect only what a feature needs, define retention limits, and provide clear user controls.

## Human need domains

- Everyday errands and local services: discover nearby providers, compare options, order goods, and arrange pickup or delivery where supported.
- Health and care: provide reliable general information, preparation and navigation support, and access to appropriate care without diagnosing or replacing clinicians.
- Learning and personal development: adaptive tutoring, language learning, and practical skill development.
- Work and livelihood: job discovery, productivity, small-business tools, and access to professional services.
- Home and daily life: planning, household assistance, visual product previews, and informed purchasing.
- Safety and digital confidence: scam awareness, account security guidance, and understandable privacy controls.
- Accessibility and communication: multilingual, voice-friendly, and assistive experiences.

## Delivery rule

Build in verified increments. Every feature must have a defined user problem, expected benefit, data requirements, risk assessment, acceptance tests, and a clear statement of what is and is not automated. Do not claim a feature is operational until its end-to-end path has been implemented and tested.

## Success measures

Evaluate outcomes such as task completion rate, time saved, successful access to services, user-reported effort, accessibility, reliability, privacy incidents, and transparent total cost. Do not optimize solely for session length, clicks, or AI usage.
